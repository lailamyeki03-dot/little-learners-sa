About Activities For Parents

Welcome, Little Learners!

We believe every child can learn through play, creativity, exploration and loving support.

🎨 Try These Learning Activities

1. Colour Hunt

Find three safe objects that are red, yellow or blue.

2. Count and Clap

Count from one to five while clapping your hands.

3. Story Time

Read a picture book together and talk about the pictures.

💛 A Note for Parents

Everyday moments can become learning opportunities. Talk, sing, count, read and play together.
